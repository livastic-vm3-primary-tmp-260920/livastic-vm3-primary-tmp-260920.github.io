Problem 1

Duplicate versus legitimate multi-event records

An exchange import may duplicate an already synced trade. Conversely, several ERC-20 Transfer logs can have one transaction hash but distinct log indexes. Removing them based on hash alone can destroy real economic records. We flag ambiguities rather than deleting data.

Problem 2

Missing acquisition and transfer counterparty

When purchase history is absent, the correct outcome is an evidence checklist—not a fabricated date, market price or acquisition cost. Withdrawal and incoming rows can be plausible counterparts, but equal amounts and timestamps alone do not prove the same beneficial owner.
